sequenceDiagram
    autonumber

    actor U as User
    participant APP as UPI App
    participant CL as NPCI Common Library
    participant PSP as PSP
    participant NPCI as NPCI Switch
    participant BANK as Payer Bank
    participant CBS as Bank CBS
    participant M as Merchant

    Note over U,APP: 1. QR & VALIDATION

    U->>APP: Scan QR
    APP->>APP: Decode + validate QR
    APP->>PSP: Resolve VPA
    PSP->>NPCI: ReqValAdd
    NPCI-->>PSP: Verified payee
    PSP-->>APP: Verified name

    Note over U,CL: 2. PIN & CREDENTIAL PROTECTION

    U->>APP: Confirm payment
    APP->>CL: Request credential
    CL->>CL: Generate transaction digest
    CL->>CL: AES-GCM encrypt digest
    CL->>CL: RSA-OAEP encrypt credential
    CL-->>APP: Opaque credential

    Note over APP,NPCI: 3. SECURE TRANSMISSION

    APP->>PSP: Encrypted payment request
    PSP->>NPCI: ReqPay over mTLS
    NPCI->>NPCI: Validate + risk checks

    Note over NPCI,BANK: 4. BANK AUTHENTICATION

    NPCI->>BANK: Debit request
    BANK->>BANK: Authenticate PIN
    BANK->>CBS: Balance + debit
    CBS-->>BANK: Debit confirmed
    BANK-->>NPCI: DebitSuccess

    Note over NPCI,CBS: 5. CREDIT & RESPONSE

    NPCI->>CBS: Credit payee
    CBS-->>NPCI: Credit confirmed
    NPCI-->>PSP: RespPay SUCCESS
    PSP-->>APP: Transaction SUCCESS

    Note over APP,M: 6. COMPLETION

    APP->>M: Signed transaction response
    APP->>U: Payment Successful
